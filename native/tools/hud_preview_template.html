HUD Desk Preview

This replays the real output of our C worker (hud-worker --dump), call by call, through a simulated HUD terminal. The terminal has images and text areas per handle, pen colours, ImageRemoveAllItems and SwitchVisibility. Timing follows a simulated round trip, with at most 3 calls waiting for an answer and 48 per batch. Drive story is a two-minute drive: the iDrive menu settings, the driving mode and the factory navigation change, and the worker's own scene logic decides what the HUD shows. Speed camera replays the scripted camera pass (500 m before to 50 m after it), alone and next to the Sport Bar.
